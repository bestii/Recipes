---
title: Shan Geo Chicken Curry
ingredients:
  - 1200 g chicken, cleaned (800 g–1.2 kg both work)
  - 4 tbsp cooking oil
  - 4 medium onions (about 400 g), sliced
  - 2 green chillies, chopped
  - 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  - 1½ tsp salt
  - ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  - 2 tbsp coriander powder
  - ¾ tbsp chilli powder
  - 1 tbsp chicken masala
  - 1 tomato, chopped
  - 1½ cups (360 ml) hot water
  - 2 sprigs curry leaves
  - ½ tsp black pepper powder
  - ½ cup thick coconut milk (optional)
  - A few shallots, dried red chillies, curry leaves and oil, for the tadka (optional)
---

1. Heat 4 tbsp of oil in a pan. Add the sliced onions, chopped green chillies and ginger-garlic paste with 1 tsp of the salt, and sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
2. Reduce the flame to low, then add the turmeric powder, coriander powder, chilli powder and chicken masala.
3. Sauté for about 1 minute so the raw flavour of the masalas is gone.
4. Add the chopped tomato, then the chicken along with the remaining ½ tsp salt.
5. Sauté the onions, chicken and tomatoes on a high flame for about 1 minute, until the masala is spread across the chicken.
6. Pour in the hot water. Once it starts boiling, set the flame to medium, close the pan and cook for about 10 minutes.
7. Sauté the chicken a little so it doesn't stick to the bottom, then cover and cook for another 10 minutes.
8. Add the curry leaves and the black pepper powder.
9. For a richer curry, stir in the thick coconut milk (optional).
10. For the tadka, heat a little oil and fry the shallots, dried red chillies and curry leaves until fragrant, then pour it over the curry(OPtional).
