---
title: Cacio e Pepe
ingredients:
  - 200 g spaghetti or tonnarelli
  - 2 tsp black peppercorns, coarsely cracked
  - 80 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  - Salt for the pasta water
---

1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve a mug of the cooking water before draining.
2. Toast the cracked pepper in a dry pan over medium heat for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
3. Add a splash of pasta water to the pan and swirl so the pepper doesn't scorch.
4. Take the pan off the heat. Add the pasta and toss, then add the Pecorino in handfuls, loosening with pasta water until the sauce turns glossy and clings.
5. Serve at once, with more pepper and Pecorino on top.
