---
title: Banana Bread
ingredients:
  - 3 very ripe bananas
  - 100 g unsalted butter, melted
  - 120 g light brown sugar
  - 2 eggs
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 200 g plain flour
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - A pinch of salt
  - 60 g walnuts, roughly chopped
---

1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a 900 g loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl, then stir in the melted butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla.
3. Fold in the flour, baking soda and salt until just combined, then stir through the walnuts.
4. Scrape into the tin and bake for 50–60 minutes, until a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.
5. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.
