---
title: Caramelized Onion and Garlic Spaghetti
ingredients:
  - 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  - 4 garlic cloves, minced
  - 2 tbsp chilli crisp
  - 1 cup cream or coconut milk, at room temperature
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce (optional)
  - ½ cup fresh Parmesan cheese, grated
  - 8 oz (225 g) pasta of your choice
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp paprika
  - 1 tsp garlic powder
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1 tsp black pepper
  - 1 tsp Italian seasoning
---

1. Slice the onion, mince the garlic and measure out all the seasonings. Bring the cream or coconut milk to room temperature.
2. Heat the butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onions and cook for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden and jammy. If they start sticking, add a splash of water.
3. While the onions caramelize, boil a pot of salted water and cook the pasta until al dente. Drain, reserving some of the pasta water.
4. Once the onions are deeply golden, stir in the garlic and all the seasonings and let them bloom for a minute.
5. Add the chilli crisp and sauté for a minute, then pour in the cream and simmer for a few minutes before stirring in the grated Parmesan.
6. Add the pasta to the sauce and toss to coat, loosening with the reserved pasta water if it is too thick — you want it silky, not sticky.
7. Serve hot with an extra spoon of chilli crisp on top, and a sprinkle of fresh parsley if you like.
