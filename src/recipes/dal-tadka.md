---
title: Dal Tadka
ingredients:
  - 200 g red lentils, rinsed
  - 1 tsp ground turmeric
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 2 tbsp ghee
  - 1 tsp cumin seeds
  - 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  - 1 dried red chilli
  - 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  - A handful of coriander, chopped
---

1. Simmer the lentils with the turmeric, salt and 800 ml water for 20 minutes, until soft and porridge-like. Top up with hot water if it thickens too much.
2. Heat the ghee in a small pan. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle and darken.
3. Add the garlic and dried chilli and fry until the garlic is golden, then take the pan off the heat and stir in the chilli powder.
4. Pour the hot tadka over the dal, stir once, and scatter with coriander.
